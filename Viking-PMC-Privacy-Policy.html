This Privacy Policy describes how Viking PMC Operations Bot (the “Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when used on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on which features are used, the Bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames, server and role IDs, command submissions, and information voluntarily submitted in reports or forms, including attendance, training results, qualifications, promotions, and personnel records. The Bot does not request Discord passwords or payment card information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate Bot commands, verify role-based permissions, manage reports and personnel records, synchronize authorized records with connected Google Sheets, maintain audit records, troubleshoot errors, and prevent misuse.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the Bot's hosting environment, application database, and Google Sheets configured by the server's authorized operators. Records are retained as needed for operational and recordkeeping purposes or until removed by authorized administrators, subject to applicable obligations and backups.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

Information may be processed through Discord, the hosting provider, and Google services used for spreadsheet synchronization. Authorized server staff may access records according to their roles. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Access and Controls

Access to administrative functions is restricted using Discord roles and configured permissions. Server administrators and authorized leadership are responsible for managing access to server channels and connected spreadsheets.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as restricted credentials and role-based access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit sensitive information that is unnecessary for the Bot's functions.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact the Bot operator to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your Discord account. Requests may be subject to identity verification, server recordkeeping needs, and applicable law. Deleting a Discord message may not automatically delete previously synchronized records.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly seek information from children below the applicable minimum age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Bot features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.
